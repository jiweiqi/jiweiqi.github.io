Proceedings of the Combustion Institute, 2017. Journal article.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.proci.2016.05.043

## Research summary

Measures ignition delays of a fully blended low-octane light naphtha fuel.

测量低辛烷值全调和轻石脑油的点火延迟。

Keywords: ignition delay, naphtha, fuel kinetics
