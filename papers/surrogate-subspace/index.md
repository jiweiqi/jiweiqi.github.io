# Propagation of Kinetic Uncertainty through Surrogate Subspace in Combustion Simulations

Weiqi Ji, Jiaxing Wang, Bin Yang, Zhuyin Ren, Chung K. Law

10th U.S. National Combustion Meeting, 2017. Conference paper.

Canonical page: https://jiweiqi.github.io/papers/surrogate-subspace/

## Research summary

Explores a low-rank surrogate subspace for propagating kinetic uncertainty, using neural response surfaces and genetic optimization.

Keywords: surrogate subspaces, uncertainty propagation, dimension reduction
